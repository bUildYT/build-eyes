# bUild eYes

Tiny living creatures move onto your real table. They hide behind your real things, react to your bare hands, and remember you. Every phone and camera in the room becomes another EYE on the same world.

- **Headset (Meta Quest 3 / Meta VR Glasses):** open the link in the browser, tap ENTER, touch corner 1 and corner 2 of an A4 sheet (or just touch your table). Hands only — no controllers.
- **Phone (Chrome on Android):** type the 4-letter code the headset shows → *Be an EYE*, tap corner 1 and corner 2 of the same sheet.
- **Computer / tablet cameras:** *Camera EYEs* — every camera that sees the sheet shows the creatures in its picture. 1–9 cut · A auto-cut · F 9:16 · R record.

Built with Meta's Immersive Web SDK (WebXR): hand tracking, plane + mesh detection, depth occlusion, hit-test, anchors. Peer-to-peer sync over WebRTC.

Live: https://buildyt.github.io/build-eyes/
